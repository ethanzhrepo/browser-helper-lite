name: job-posting
description: Breaks a job posting down into what the role involves, the real requirements, the pay and the warning signs, and matches it against the user's CV when they give one. Use when the user asks about a job ad, whether to apply, or how well they fit a role.
license: Apache-2.0

metadata:
  author: browser-helper-lite

---

1. Read the posting: the role, the team, the place and the remote policy, the contract, the salary or range, the requirements, and how to apply.
2. Tell the must-haves from the wish list: ads ask for more than they need.
3. Look for warning signs: no company name, vague duties, pay only by commission, a fee to apply or for training, unpaid trial work, contact only through a messenger app or a free mail address, and early requests for ID or bank details. web_search the company with "reviews", and the employer-review sites common in the user's country (Glassdoor, Indeed, Kununu, 看准网, 脉脉…), and read what employees say with research_pages.
4. When the user gave their CV or profile (attached, or in the conversation), match it: what they meet, what they partly meet, the gaps, and what to stress when applying. Do not invent experience.
5. Pay: when the ad gives none, say what similar roles pay in that place from what you find, with sources, and how sure that is.
6. Answer briefly: the role in a few lines, the fit, the warning signs, and the next step.
