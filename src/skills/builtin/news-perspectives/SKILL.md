---
name: news-perspectives
description: Compares how several outlets report the same news story, what they agree on, where they differ, and what each leaves out. Use when the user asks for other views on a story, whether coverage is biased, or what different sources say about an event.
license: Apache-2.0
compatibility: Written for Browser Helper Lite. Uses its web_search and research_pages tools.
metadata:
  author: browser-helper-lite
  version: "1.0"
---

1. Pin the story down: the event, the date, and the facts in dispute. Start from the article the user is on, if any.
2. Find coverage from outlets of different leanings, and from different countries when that matters, plus news agencies and primary sources (official statements, documents, data). web_search the key names with the date, and read the reports together with research_pages.
3. Compare: the facts all agree on; where they differ (figures, the order of events, who is quoted); the framing (the headline's wording, what comes first, whose voices are left out); and what has changed since the first reports.
4. Answer with what is established, then a table: outlet, how it frames the story, what it stresses or leaves out, link and time. Describe framing neutrally and let the user judge.
5. Say how fresh the information is, and when the story is still developing.
