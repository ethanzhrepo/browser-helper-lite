# Where to look, in any country

Sites come and go: check that a site is still active and that its review is recent. Search in the local language. Testers that measure come first; shops' own ratings and social media show what owners live with. Sites marked "forum" or "social" are opened in the tab and only read.

## By category
- Phones: GSMArena (gsmarena.com, specs and reviews), DXOMARK (dxomark.com, camera and display tests).
- Laptops: Notebookcheck (notebookcheck.net, measured tests).
- TVs, monitors, headphones, vacuums: RTINGS (rtings.com).
- Cameras and lenses: DPReview (dpreview.com).
- Elsewhere: the national consumer association, the local Amazon or the leading marketplace, a local price-comparison site, and the biggest local tech forum. Search "<category> review" and "best <category> <year>" in the local language.
